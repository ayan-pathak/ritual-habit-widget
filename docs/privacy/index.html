Privacy

Ritual keeps one thing: which days you kept a practice. Everything below is the whole of it.

Without an account, nothing leaves your phone

Every ritual you keep is stored on the device, in the app's own storage, as a name, a time of day, a colour, and a list of days. An install that is never signed in never sends any of it anywhere, and works with no network at all.

With an account, we store exactly this

  • Your email address and a user ID, held by Firebase Authentication so a new phone can find your rituals. If you sign in with Google or Apple, they tell us the same two things and nothing more; we never see your password.
  • Your rituals, held in Cloud Firestore at users/<your user ID>/habits/<ritual ID>: the name you typed, the time of day, the colour, the day it was created, and the list of days you marked.

That is the complete list. There is no profile, no contacts, no location, no photos, no device identifier, no address book, no advertising ID.

Who can read it

Only you. The database rule is one line — a ritual document is readable and writable only by the signed-in account whose ID is in its path — and there is no query shape that reaches across accounts. Nothing is shared, published, or sold, and nothing is handed to anyone else except the two services below that hold it on our behalf.

The services involved

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your email, user ID and rituals. Their handling is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing handles the one-time unlock. The purchase happens inside Google Play; Ritual is told whether you own it and is never given your payment details, card, or address.
  • Instagram is only ever handed an image you chose to share, at the moment you tap Share. It is not given your rituals, your account, or anything else.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Ritual has no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Nobody is counting how often you open it, because nothing is watching.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Deleting a ritual in the app removes it from your phone and marks it deleted in the cloud copy so your other devices stop showing it. Deleting your account removes the account and the rituals under it. There is no backup we keep afterwards.

Getting rid of it

Delete your account and everything in it — the page explains both ways. Uninstalling the app removes the on-device copy; if you were signed in, the cloud copy survives until you delete the account.

Children

Ritual is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the history is public in the repository the app is built from.

Contact

Questions, or a request about your data: you@example.com.

Before you submit to Google Play: replace you@example.com on this page and on the deletion page with a real address you read. Play checks that the contact works.
