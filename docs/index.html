The part other apps get wrong

A streak resets. A year doesn't.

Most habit apps treat one bad Tuesday like a crime. You miss once, the counter drops to zero, three weeks of effort vanish in a single notification, and you quit.

Ritual keeps every square you've ever filled. Miss a day and there's a gap in the grid. That's all. Tomorrow is a fresh square sitting right next to all the ones you already earned.

Real life has fevers, flights and weddings. Your year can handle them, and Mochi has no opinion about yesterday.

Meet Mochi

A cat who's quietly proud of you.

Mochi is calm while today is still open and thrilled the moment you keep it. He doesn't send notifications. He doesn't lecture. He has no sad face, because there's no version of this where you're in trouble. He just lives on your home screen, noticing every day you show up.

Hand drawn as vectors, so he stays crisp on a 24 pixel badge and on a full story card. Tap him.

Before you download

The questions everyone asks.

What happens if I miss a day?
Nothing resets. That day stays empty and every other square stays exactly where it was.
Can I track more than one habit?
Your first ritual is free. A one time $4.99 unlocks as many as you want.
Do I need an account?
Yes, one sign in on the first launch, with Google, Apple or an email address. It exists so your year survives a new phone. Nothing else in the app asks you for anything.
Where is my data stored?
Every square lives on your phone. Because you're signed in, your rituals are also mirrored to your account so a new phone picks them up. That's a name, a colour, a time of day and a list of dates, and nothing else. The full list is here.
Will it send me notifications?
Never. The widget on your home screen is the reminder.
Is it on iPhone?
Not yet, it's being ported. Leave your email and you'll hear first.
Why is it open source?
So you don't have to take anyone's word for "no trackers". Anyone can check for themselves.
