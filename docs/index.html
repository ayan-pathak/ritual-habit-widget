Why this one lasts

Most habit apps get deleted in week three.

Not because people stop caring. Because opening an app to record that you did a thing is itself a habit, and one broken streak makes the whole thing feel pointless. Ritual removes both problems.

One

You never have to remember it

The widget sits on your home screen between your camera and your messages. You see your year every time you unlock your phone, and the button marks today from right there. There is nothing to open and nothing to remember.

Two

A missed day costs you one square

No chain to break, no reset to zero, no screen telling you that you failed. The days you did keep stay exactly where they are, which is the difference between a slip and a reason to quit.

Three

You can finally see the work

A number in a list is forgettable. Two hundred filled squares is something you look at in December and think, I actually did that. It is the same reason people keep a wall calendar.

Before you download

The questions people ask first.

What happens if I miss a day?
Nothing resets. That day stays empty and every other square stays exactly where it was. You pick up tomorrow with everything you have already built still intact.
Will it nag me?
No. Ritual sends no notifications at all. The widget on your home screen is the only reminder, and it is one you chose to put there.
Is $4.99 a subscription?
No. It is one payment that never renews, and it unlocks unlimited habits on every device you sign in on. Your first habit is free whether or not you ever pay.
Do I need an account?
Yes, one sign in on the first launch with Google, Apple or an email address. It exists so your year survives a new phone. Nothing else in the app asks you for anything.
Where is my data stored?
Every square lives on your phone, and your rituals are mirrored to your account so a new phone picks them up. That is a name, a colour, a time of day and a list of dates, and nothing else. The full list is here.
Can I track more than one habit?
Yes, as many as you want once you have unlocked it. Each one gets its own colour, its own time of day and its own widget.
Why should I trust it with my data?
You do not have to take anyone's word for it. The whole app is open source, so anyone can read exactly what it does.
