Account deletion

Delete your account

Everything held under your account, gone, with nothing kept afterwards. Two taps inside the app, or one email if you have already uninstalled it.

What goes

  • Your account itself: the email address and the user ID that Firebase Authentication holds.
  • Every ritual stored under it: the names, the times of day, the colours, and every day you marked.

There is no backup retained after that, and no waiting period we hold it through. It is removed within 30 days of the request, and normally within a day or two.

What stays

  • The copy on your phone. Ritual works with no account at all, so deleting the account leaves every square on the device exactly where it is. Uninstalling the app removes that copy.
  • Your purchase. The one-time unlock belongs to your Google Play account, not to your Ritual account, so it survives and restores on a new install. We never held your payment details in the first place.

In the app, in two taps

Open Ritual, go to the Account screen, and tap Delete your account at the bottom. It asks once to be sure, then removes the account and everything stored under it straight away. No email, no waiting, and nobody to ask.

Or by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email pathak.ayan@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with Delete my Ritual account as the subject. Sending it from that address is what proves it is yours; nothing else is needed. You will get a reply confirming it is done.

Just signing out instead

If you only want to stop syncing, sign out on the Account screen. Every ritual stays on the phone and nothing more is written to the cloud copy. Your account and its rituals stay where they are until you ask for them to go.
