```
Most habit apps get deleted in week three. Not because you stopped caring, but
because opening an app to record that you did the thing is itself a habit. And
one broken streak makes the whole thing feel pointless.

Ritual removes both problems.

YOUR YEAR, ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Ritual is a widget first and an app second. Your whole year sits between your

WATCH IT FILL UP
Mark today and the whole card flips to ink. That flip is the reward, and it

ONE FOR EACH THING YOU ARE BUILDING

SOMETHING TO SHOW FOR IT
One tap turns your year into a story card for Instagram: the real grid, the
real number, nothing invented. Telling people is the oldest trick there is for
actually keeping a habit.

A CAT CALLED MOCHI
There is a cat on the card. He rests while today is unmarked and he is pleased
the moment you tap. He never guilts you about a missed day, because the empty
square has already said everything there is to say.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO
No notifications, ever. No ads. No trackers and no analytics. No subscription.
It works with no signal at all, because every square lives on your phone.

PRICING
Your first ritual is free and stays free, with no trial clock and no card.
One payment of $4.99 unlocks unlimited rituals on every device you sign in on,
for good. There is no monthly anything.

OPEN SOURCE
The whole app is public, so you do not have to take anyone's word for "no
```

## App content answers

**Target audience**: 18 and over. Anything below that pulls in extra Families
policy requirements for no gain here.

**Ads**: No.

**Content rating**: no violence, no sexual content, no profanity, no
controlled substances, no gambling, no user-to-user communication, no
user-generated content shared inside the app, no location sharing. Sharing a
story card hands an image to another app through the system share sheet; there
is no social layer in Ritual. That lands at Everyone.

**Data safety**

| Data | Collected | Shared | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email address | Yes | No | Yes | Account management |
| User IDs | Yes | No | Yes | Account management, app functionality |
| Other user-generated content (ritual names, colours, times of day, the days you marked) | Yes | No | Yes | App functionality |

Encrypted in transit: yes. Users can request deletion: yes, in the app and at
https://ayan-pathak.github.io/ritual-habit-widget/delete-account/

**App access**: sign-in is required on the first launch, so this cannot be left
blank. Create one real account, then give the reviewer its email and password
under "All functionality is restricted", with the note: *Sign in with the email
option on the first screen. No other steps are needed.* A reviewer who cannot
get past the first screen rejects the app.

**Financial features**: none. The one-time unlock is Google Play Billing, not a
financial product.

## Before the first upload

- **Play App Signing SHA-1.** After the bundle is uploaded, copy it from Setup
  → App integrity into the Firebase project. Play re-signs the app with its own
  key, so Google Sign-In fails in every distributed build until Firebase knows
  that fingerprint.
- **Payments profile.** Setup → Payments profile has to clear before an in-app
  product can be created or sold.
- **Order of operations.** Upload a bundle to a track first. Play will not let
  you create `ritual_unlimited` until it has seen an artifact carrying the
  billing permission.
